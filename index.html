// ENGINEERING BACKGROUND

Crafting Intelligent Robotics & Software Systems

I am a Computer Engineering student at University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Taxila. My passion lies in creating high-reliability autonomous systems, embedded electronics, real-time control loops, and computer-vision powered web & mobile applications.

For my Final Year Project (FYP), I am architecting an AI-Powered Cooperative Drone Swarm system designed for autonomous area coverage, search-and-rescue operations, and real-time object detection using ROS2, ArduPilot SITL, Gazebo, and YOLO.
